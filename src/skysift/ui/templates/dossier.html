The formulas behind the drawings

  • Star colour by Teff: above 5,500 K pale yellow, above 4,500 K light orange, above 3,900 K orange, otherwise red-orange.
  • Planet radius Rp = k R★, with k the fitted radius ratio; the disc colour by Teq: under 200 K ice blue, under 320 K sea green, under 700 K sand, otherwise rust.
  • Orbit a = (G M★ P² / 4π²)1/3; luminosity L = R★² (Teff / 5,772 K)⁴; starlight received S = L / a².
  • Teq = Teff (R★ / 2a)1/2 (1 − A)1/4 with albedo A = 0.3 and full heat redistribution.
