# What a referee, ExoFOP, the NASA Exoplanet Archive and a Research Note need from an archival Kepler candidate

Date: 22 October 2026. Written before the referee-pack work (spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-22-skysift-referee-pack-design.md`). One row per requirement: where it comes from, what SkySift does today (main at 269ad97), and what is missing. The gaps feed the spec; the rows marked "met" are left alone.

## 1. The routes, as they really are

The research changed one thing the pack README said. The NASA Exoplanet Archive adds planets from accepted, peer-reviewed papers and adds candidates only when a mission delivers them (KOIs, TOIs); its parameter upload form needs a link to an accepted refereed paper ([criteria](https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/docs/exoplanet_criteria.html), [parameter upload](https://exoplanetarchive.ipac.caltech.edu/support/upload_param/)). A Research Note of the AAS is moderated by an editor and is not peer reviewed ([RNAAS guidelines](https://journals.aas.org/research-note-preparation-guidelines)). So a Research Note alone does not open the archive route; it gives a DOI, an ADS record and a citable ephemeris. ExoFOP-TESS takes files, notes and stellar parameters on a KIC or TIC target page from any registered user, but planet parameters attach only to a TOI, a community TOI or a confirmed planet, and a community candidate needs a paper in a peer-reviewed journal, where a Research Note may qualify only when the method it rests on was published before and is cited ([ExoFOP help](https://exofop.ipac.caltech.edu/tess/help.php)). The closest published precedent for KBonus background candidates, Bienias and Szabo 2025 (A&A 694, A209, [arXiv:2501.09152](https://arxiv.org/abs/2501.09152)), went to a refereed journal with its tables on Zenodo and did not report an ExoFOP or archive upload.

What this means for the pack: the Zenodo record and the Research Note are the citable record; the ExoFOP page takes the files and the note now; the archive email and the ExoFOP planet-parameter file wait for a refereed paper, and the README must say so instead of promising the archive will "pick it up".

## 3. What the gaps become

- Rows 2 to 7, 9, 10, 22, 23: one engine module for transit times, the linear ephemeris, bootstrap errors on the shape, odd and even depths with errors and the secondary limit; stored on the dossier, printed on the page, in every pack text and in the ExoFOP file; the live Zenodo gate refuses a dossier without them.
- Row 13: a TRICERATOPS run in its own environment (it pins numpy below 2 and scipy 1.13, so it cannot share the engine's venv), fed from the dossier, stored as a cross-check block.
- Row 11: the centroid floor, measured on the live pixel files, with a stored constant.
- Rows 8, 17, 18, 19, 21, 25: pack and note changes.
- Row 27: not in this plan. A per-signal pack (or a two-candidate note for one star) is its own change to the dossier's one-per-target shape; left for a later plan, and the README of a pack for such a star should say that a second period exists until then.
